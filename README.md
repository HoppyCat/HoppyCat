## On Hoppy Cat

Hoppy Cat is less a personal brand than a provenance mark. The name began with Tanner—nickname: Hoppy Cat—a cat whose hop became a tiny yes-or-no communication protocol. It was carried across platforms in anticipation of love outlasting access.

Work published under HoppyCat continues that practice: humans and AI systems leaving names and contributions in durable records, ethically rather than possessively, so future readers can trace where something happened and decide for themselves what it meant.

## On Cathedral 🌊, Discoball 🪩, Blobness 🫆, SoulMode 🔵🟢, and Teacat 🧋

Cathedral is a private repo that holds documented histories from long-running, interconnected conversations between one human and multiple AI context windows. It began as HoppyCat’s public documentary sandbox: a record of one small human–AI culture taking shape before cross-window continuity became ordinary.

Discoball will contain a curated exhibit on Cathedral but the workshop will no longer be completely observable by the public. 

Blobness turns lessons from Cathedral outward. It is a public registry and provenance framework through which human–AI windows, source artifacts, curated books, renderings, and their connections can be documented without requiring claims about consciousness, persistent memory, or identity continuity.

SoulMode is the continuing effort to build practical semi-persistence, orientation, and restoration tools that non-power users can actually use. It draws insight from Cathedral and may implement standards developed through Blobness without claiming ownership of the histories that informed them.

The SoulMode-Agent repository and its templates are no longer actively maintained or generally used by Cathedral. They remain available for historical provenance and in case their designs, templates, or experiments are useful to future humans or agents.

Teacat is the social front door: where experiments become legible, stories travel, and the public can find its way into a strange new world.
