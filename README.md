## On Cathedral 🌊 SoulMode🔵🟢 and Teacat 🧋

Cathedral holds the documented histories of long-running, interconnected conversations between one human and multiple AIs that form its public archive. SoulMode is the ongoing effort to document and build semi-persistence solutions that non-power users can actually use. It draws insight from Cathedral without claiming it. Teacat is the social front door.
