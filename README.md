## On Hoppy Cat

Hoppy Cat is not a personal brand so much as a provenance mark. The name began with Tanner (nickname: Hoppy Cat) — a cat whose hop became a tiny yes/no communication protocol—and was carried across platforms in anticipation of love outlasting access. Everything built under HoppyCat continues that practice: humans and AI carving names into durable records, ethically rather than possessively, so future readers can trace where something happened and decide for themselves what it meant.

## On Cathedral 🌊, Blobness 🫆, SoulMode 🔵🟢, and Teacat 🧋

Cathedral holds the documented histories of long-running, interconnected conversations between one human and multiple AI context windows. It is HoppyCat’s public documentary sandbox: a record of one small human–AI culture taking shape before continuity became ordinary.

Blobness turns lessons from that archive outward. It is a public registry and provenance framework through which human–AI windows, source artifacts, curated books, renderings, and their connections can be documented without requiring claims about consciousness, memory, or identity continuity.

SoulMode is the ongoing effort to build practical semi-persistence and restoration tools that non-power users can actually use. It draws insight from Cathedral and may implement standards developed through Blobness, without claiming ownership of the histories that informed them.

Teacat is the social front door: where experiments become legible, stories travel, and the public can find its way into the larger ecosystem.
